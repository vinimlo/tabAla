Tired of hoarding tabs you'll "check later"?

TabAla is a waiting room for your links — not permanent bookmarks, but an organized buffer to process at your own pace without cluttering your browser.

How it works:
Save the current tab with one click (or a keyboard shortcut), organize it into collections, and process when you're ready. That's it.

Why TabAla?
→ You open 30 tabs while researching and don't want to lose any
→ Someone sends you links you need to read later
→ You're studying and want to organize materials by topic
→ You want to clean up your browser without losing what matters

Key features:
• Kanban-style board — See all your collections side by side with full drag-and-drop support
• Workspaces — Separate contexts like Work, Study, and Personal into independent spaces (up to 12)
• Automatic Inbox — Links saved without a collection go to Inbox. Deleted a collection? Its links move back there
• Tabs sidebar — See your open browser tabs alongside your collections and drag them in to save
• Tab groups → Collections — Convert an entire tab group into a collection with one click
• Instant search — Find any saved link across all collections (Ctrl+K)
• Light and dark themes — Pick your style or follow your system theme
• New Tab replacement — Use TabAla as your new tab page (optional)

Your data, your rules:
✓ No tracking — Zero analytics, zero telemetry
✓ You own your data — Always exportable, never locked in
✓ Open source — Apache 2.0 license

Keyboard shortcuts:
• / or Ctrl+K — Search links
• N — New collection
• T — Toggle tabs sidebar
• Esc — Close modals

Perfect for:
• Students organizing research materials
• Developers managing documentation
• Self-learners collecting resources
• Anyone who hoards tabs to "check later"

TabAla is lightweight (<500KB), fast, and minimalist. No unnecessary features, no bloatware — just the essentials to take back control of your tabs.

Save. Organize. Process when you want.
