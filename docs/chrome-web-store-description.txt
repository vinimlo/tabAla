Tired of hoarding tabs you'll "check later"?

TabAla is a waiting room for your links — not permanent bookmarks, but an organized buffer you actually get through, at your own pace, without cluttering your browser.

How it works:
Save the current tab with one click (or a keyboard shortcut) and organize it into collections. Every new tab then suggests what to open next. Done with a link? Mark it completed and it leaves the board.

Why TabAla?
→ You open 30 tabs while researching and don't want to lose any
→ Someone sends you links you need to read later
→ You're studying and want to organize materials by topic
→ You save everything and never get back to it

Key features:
• Now — Each new tab opens on one suggestion of what to read next, with the time it takes and the next ones beside it
• Focus — A timed session of 15, 30 or 60 minutes over your collections. A started session follows you across tabs
• Completed, not deleted — Finished links leave the board and stay in your history, with undo. You can also snooze a link or mark it as reference
• Triage — Links untouched for months, skipped again and again or snoozed too often come back one at a time: still worth it, discard, reference or already done
• Command palette — Search every workspace, run commands and act on a link (open, complete, snooze, move) without leaving the keyboard
• Search by topic (optional) — Chrome's on-device translator lets a search in your language find links titled in English
• Kanban-style board — See all your collections side by side with drag-and-drop
• Workspaces — Separate contexts like Work, Study, and Personal into independent spaces (up to 12)
• Automatic Inbox — Links saved without a collection go to Inbox. Deleted a collection? Its links move back there
• Open tabs — See the tabs open in your browser from the side rail and drag them in to save
• Tab groups → Collections — Convert an entire tab group into a collection with one click
• Light and dark themes — Pick your style or follow your system theme
• New Tab replacement — Use TabAla as your new tab page (optional)

Your data, your rules:
✓ No server — Links, usage and search all stay in your browser
✓ No tracking — Zero analytics, zero telemetry
✓ Learning you control — TabAla notices which saved links you open, and for how long, to suggest what's next. Turn it off and clear that data in Settings
✓ You own your data — Always exportable, never locked in
✓ Open source — Apache 2.0 license

Keyboard shortcuts:
• / or Ctrl+K (⌘K on Mac) — Search and commands
• N — New collection
• F — Focus
• T — Open tabs
• Esc — Close

Perfect for:
• Students organizing research materials
• Developers managing documentation
• Self-learners collecting resources
• Anyone who hoards tabs to "check later"

TabAla stays small and fast. No accounts, no ads, nothing to sign up for.
